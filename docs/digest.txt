1. BROTLI EXPLOITATION HITS MAGENTO STORES
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, e-commerce]
   Latest developments: CISA confirmed active exploitation of
   CVE-2026-111497, the Brotli flaw researchers call Node2Shell,
   Broccoli2Shell, and HTTP2Shell, and two honeypot operators
   logged attempts, one of them firing a broken exploit; Sophos
   published its own confirmation of exploitation in the wild;
   and separately attackers breached hundreds of Adobe Commerce
   and Magento stores through CVE-2026-48356 in a campaign
   researchers named PolyShell.
   CVE-2026-111497 is an unchecked return value rated 9.2 in
   Google's Brotli compression library that runs an attacker's
   code in any application decompressing attacker-supplied
   Brotli data, among them Node.js, .NET, PowerShell, Bun, and
   the Python brotli packages. Update the bundling runtime and
   patch Adobe Commerce and Magento, where CVE-2026-48356 allows
   an unrestricted file upload.

9. VOLT TYPHOON'S SILENT TRAIN MASKS ZERO-DAYS
   Nation-State Activity · [apt, zero-day,
   critical-infrastructure]
   Latest developments: Researchers published the first concrete
   account of the tradecraft United States officials have called
   sophisticated for years, describing Silent Train, a framework
   that strings together devices Volt Typhoon compromises on
   demand into rotating relay chains and routes zero-day attacks
   on high-value systems through them, which Kim Zetter and
   BleepingComputer both detailed on October 28, 2026.
   Volt Typhoon is the Chinese state-sponsored group that United
   States agencies accuse of pre-positioning inside power,
   water, and communications networks. Silent Train hides the
   origin of its intrusions behind freshly hijacked intermediate
   hosts, so defenders should hunt for inbound sessions relayed
   through small office routers and other edge devices instead
   of relying on known command-and-control addresses.
