   multi-agent penetration-testing framework, against more than
   200 organizations across 14 countries; Kaspersky's Securelist
   published an analysis the same day of attacks on educational
   institutions in which the CyberStrikeAI framework automated

   Aur0ra ransomware group as GOLD OGRE, recovered Cursor
