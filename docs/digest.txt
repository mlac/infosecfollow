   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [cryptocurrency, breach, apt]
   Latest developments: Binance confirmed the loss at about $6.3

   systems and require out-of-band approval for withdrawal
   transactions.

   AI Security · [supply chain, backdoor, ai]

   credentials those hosts could reach.
