2. CONNECTWISE DISABLES SEVEN APPLICATION IDENTITIES
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [supply chain, breach, cloud]
   Latest developments: ConnectWise said on October 18, 2026
   that it disabled seven of its application identities, among
   them ConnectWise Cloud Backup under the identifier
   506e08f9-6e9a-4da4-942b-a099a03644ae, as its investigation
   into the unauthorized access continues.

   identities behind ConnectWise Cloud Backup, formerly SkyKick

   the service; administrators should audit sign-ins and consent
