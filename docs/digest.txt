3. TURLA'S CEREAL TOOLSET SIPHONS GOVERNMENT EMAIL
   Nation-State Activity · [apt, espionage, malware]
   Latest developments: ESET disclosed on November 10, 2026 that
   Turla, the Russia-aligned group also tracked as Snake,
   planted a previously undocumented modular .NET toolset
   researchers named Cereal on the Microsoft Exchange servers of
   two government organizations, one in Kazakhstan and one in
   Romania, and copied selected messages straight out of the
   mail flow, among them correspondence those governments
   exchanged with NATO and European Union officials.
   Cereal needs no attacker-owned command server: operators send
   instructions to the victim's own internet-facing Exchange
   server disguised as ordinary Outlook Web Access,
   Autodiscover, and ActiveSync requests, which leaves little
   outbound traffic to spot. Exchange administrators at
   government bodies should audit server-side modules and
   inspect those three request paths for anomalies.
