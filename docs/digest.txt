7. FLASH CHIP ATTACK BREAKS TPM LOCKOUT
   Platform Security · [hardware, encryption]
   Latest developments: Researchers at Technische Universität
   Berlin showed that anyone who opens the case of an AMD-based
   PC, and of some Intel PCs, can reset the TPM lockout that
   throttles PIN guessing, and in lab tests they recovered a
   four-digit Windows Hello PIN on an AMD laptop and a six-digit
   BitLocker PIN on an Intel laptop in days.
   Short Windows Hello and BitLocker PINs rely on the TPM
   refusing repeated guesses, and physical access to the flash
   chip removes that limit. Organizations issuing laptops should
   require long PINs or pre-boot passphrases and treat a lost
   machine as a machine an attacker can open.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/flash-chip-attack-bypasses-tpm-lockout.html
