   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [cryptocurrency, breach, apt]
   Latest developments: Binance confirmed the loss at about $6.3
   billion on Thursday, October 29, 2026, raising the figure
   from the $5.8 billion it was probing a day earlier, and said
   in a security update early on October 30, 2026 that a
   compromised DevOps engineer's laptop let the attacker reach a
   service container and submit unauthorized withdrawals, with
   no evidence of a stolen wallet private key; Wired reported

   that tracing firms expect billions to stay gone; the exchange
   has frozen nearly $3 billion on-chain, Tether froze 1.567

   this is the biggest cryptocurrency theft ever recorded.
   Companies holding digital assets should treat engineer
   workstations with privileged pipeline access as crown-jewel
   systems and require out-of-band approval for withdrawal
   transactions.
