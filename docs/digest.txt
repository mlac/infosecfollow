2. CISCO VPN ZERO-DAY LANDS IN A PATCH WAVE
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch]
   Latest developments: Cisco shipped fixes on October 29, 2026
   for CVE-2026-76418, rated 8.6, a flaw in the Remote Access
   SSL VPN service of ASA and FTD software that lets an
   unauthenticated remote attacker force the firewall to reload,
   the second Cisco firewall VPN zero-day under exploitation
   since August 2026; Adobe, Veeam, and Juniper Networks closed
   critical code execution, authorization bypass, and privilege
   escalation bugs the same day, led by CVE-2026-104351 at 9.9
   in Adobe Campaign Classic; and the security startup NebuSec
   disclosed a Telegram for Android flaw that ran an attacker's
   code the moment a victim answered a voice call, which
   Telegram fixed in version 12.10.4 in late September 2026.

   way to knock ASA and FTD firewalls offline, and the Telegram
   flaw needed nothing more than an ordinary account and an
   answered call. Patch the Cisco VPN flaw first, then the
   Adobe, Veeam, and Juniper releases, then move Telegram for
   Android to 12.10.4 or later.
