   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, kev]
   Latest developments: Cyber agencies in the Netherlands, the
   United States, and the United Kingdom released advisories on
   Sunday, September 27, 2026 confirming the flaws, a day after
   incident responders started warning about NetScaler Gateway;
   Help Net Security reported that attackers exploited
   CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 for weeks before disclosure

   confirmed the activity, and CISA ordered federal agencies to
   patch by Wednesday, September 30, 2026.

   an affected version should patch and hunt for webshells.
