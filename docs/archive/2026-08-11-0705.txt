2. POLISH HEAT PLANT BREACHED THROUGH PRIVATE APN
   Critical Infrastructure Security · [ics,
   critical-infrastructure, breach]
   Latest developments: CERT Polska disclosed that a December
   29, 2025 attack on a Polish combined heat and power plant
   marked the first observed case of intruders reaching an
   operational technology network through a private APN, the
   dedicated cellular link the local grid operator uses to reach
   remote equipment.
   The attackers shut down a steam turbine and the process-water
   treatment system at a plant supplying heat to roughly 50,000
   residents, with recovery starting around 7:30 a.m. while they
   remained inside the network. The same day, coordinated
   attacks struck more than 30 other renewable installations and
   a larger heat plant.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/hackers-breach-polish-power-plant.html

5. BLOCKCHAIN-BASED BOTNET C2 SPREADS
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [botnet, malware, ddos]
   Latest developments: Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 published
   back-to-back teardowns of two botnets that anchor
   command-and-control on public blockchains: the Aeternum
   loader, which routes through Polygon smart contracts, and
   Kimwolf v7, which resolves its controllers through Ethereum
   ENS with Tor as a backup while running HTTP/2 DDoS

   Both operations use decentralized infrastructure that

   provider can pull the C2 offline. Defenders should watch for
   outbound connections to blockchain RPC endpoints and ENS
   resolvers from IoT and endpoint fleets.

6. BDTHEMES SUPPLY-CHAIN HACK SPAWNS ROGUE WORDPRESS ADMINS
   Software Supply Chain · [supply-chain, wordpress, breach]
   Latest developments: Wordfence researcher Paolo Tresso
   reported that a threat actor compromised the upstream
   infrastructure of premium WordPress plugin vendor BdThemes
   and altered a remote JSON feed delivered to administrators'
   browsers so it silently creates rogue admin accounts,
   prompting the WordPress.org plugins team to disable the
   downloads.
   The attack modified no source files in the official
   WordPress.org repository, instead poisoning data the plugins
   fetch at runtime, which defeats traditional code-scanning
   defenses. Sites running BdThemes tools should audit for
   unrecognized administrator accounts.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/bdthemes-supply-chain-attack-poisons.html
