2. CLICKFIX TURNS SIGNED SIEMENS SOFTWARE INTO A LOADER
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, malware, exploited]
   Latest developments: Siemens patched CVE-2026-110936, a DLL
   sideloading flaw rated 8.4 under CVSS v4 in Siemens Software
   Center that attackers already exploit by pairing a genuine
   signed copy of the program with malicious DLLs delivered
   through ClickFix lures, and Forescout Research - Vedere Labs
   disclosed ROMULUS, a ClickFix delivery platform whose fake
   landing pages push Legion Loader and other malware.
   ClickFix tricks a user into pasting an attacker's command
   into Windows, so the victim runs the malware personally and
   sidesteps download controls. Industrial customers should
   update Siemens Software Center at once, and security teams
   should block clipboard-to-Run-dialog behavior and alert on
   it.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/clickfix-attackers-exploit-siemens.html
