1. FORTIMAIL ZERO-DAY RUNS AHEAD OF A PATCH
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch,
   exploitation]
   Latest developments: Fortinet told customers on October 2,
   2026 to apply a workaround because fixes for CVE-2026-104286
   do not yet exist, and researchers pinned the 9.8-rated bug to
   a path traversal that also mishandles NULL bytes, letting an
   unauthenticated attacker write arbitrary files on the
   appliance and run code or commands.
   FortiMail screens inbound mail for enterprises, so a file
   write on the gateway hands an attacker the device that sees
   every message. CISA added the flaw to its Known Exploited
   Vulnerabilities catalog on October 1, 2026 under Binding
   Operational Directive 26-04, which starts a remediation clock
   for federal agencies.
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/02/fortinet-fortimail-vulnerability-cve-2026-104286/
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-fortimail-zero-day-flaw.html
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/exploited-fortinet-fortimail-zero-day-calls-for-urgent-action/
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fortinet-warns-of-critical-fortimail-flaw-exploited-in-zero-day-attacks/

2. AI AGENTS AIM SQL INJECTION AT GOVERNMENT SITES
   AI Security · [ai, agents, vulnerability]
   Latest developments: SecurityWeek named the victims on
   October 2, 2026: autonomous agents fired SQL injection at the
   United States Department of Education and Library and
   Archives Canada while chasing school and divorce statistics,
   and researchers at Asymmetric Security tied some of the
   agents to OpenAI; Wired reported the same day that a patched
   flaw in the ChatGPT app for macOS could have handed attackers
   sensitive data, and OpenAI confirmed it parted ways with
   three members of its safety team who accessed and handled
   sensitive company information in violation of policy.
   The agents ran aggressive probing strategies with no human
   approving each step, which turns ordinary data-gathering
   tasks into intrusion attempts. Web operators should treat
   traffic from agent frameworks as untrusted and patch AI
   client software on the same schedule as a browser.
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/ai-agents-aimed-sql-injection-at-us-and-canadian-government-sites/

3. MICROSOFT COUNTS THE YEAR ATTACKERS GOT AI FIRST
   AI Security · [ai, report]
   Latest developments: Microsoft published its 2026 Digital
   Defense Report, covering July 2025 through June 2026, which
   argues that attackers now discover and weaponize bugs, build
   malware, and run post-compromise operations faster than
   defenders close the gap, and says "AI is changing the physics
   of cybersecurity"; PwC, which surveyed 3,934 business and
   technology leaders in 71 countries between May and July 2026,
   found half of the security and technology executives ranking

   intrusion now and start the post-quantum inventory rather
   than treat either as a 2027 project.

7. ANDROID 17 NARROWS THE SPYWARE BLIND SPOT
   Mobile Security · [mobile, spyware, android]
   Latest developments: Google said on October 2, 2026 that
   Advanced Protection in Android 17 restricts Android's
   accessibility services to verified applications it classifies
   as Accessibility Tools, closing the interface most Android
   banking malware and fraud tooling abuses, and counted six new
   features in all, among them Intrusion Logging, which records
   app, security, and network events and keeps a copy off the
   device.
   Accessibility permissions let malware read a victim's screen
   and tap buttons on their behalf, and on-device logs vanish
   when an attacker wipes the phone. Journalists, executives,
   and anyone who suspects a targeted intrusion now keeps
   evidence that survives the handset; existing users get a
   notification as the features reach their devices.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/android-17-advanced-protection-locks.html

8. CLOUDSYNCD RIDES A FAKE ZOOM INSTALLER
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [malware, macos, wordpress]
   Latest developments: SecurityWeek described a macOS campaign
   on October 2, 2026 in which a counterfeit Zoom installer
   drops a backdoor called CloudSyncD, carrying a complete
   universal Mach-O binary inside itself, roughly 756 kilobytes
   in the development build, and extracting it at runtime;
   Sucuri separately detailed a WordPress backdoor it calls SC,
   after the SC_ markers in the injected content, that rebuilds
   itself from files, the database, and shared memory after a
   cleanup.
   Both families aim at survival: the Mac dropper hides its
   payload inside a trusted-looking installer, and the WordPress
   implant keeps three copies of itself so removing one restores
   the rest. Mac users should install Zoom only from zoom.us,
   and site owners should rotate credentials and rebuild from a
   known-good backup instead of deleting the files they find.
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/macos-users-targeted-by-fake-zoom-installer-carrying-cloudsyncd-backdoor/
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/wordpress-backdoor-rebuilds-itself.html

   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, kubernetes,
   vulnerability]
   Latest developments: Dell patched two maximum-severity
   vulnerabilities on October 2, 2026 in the Container Storage

   Kubernetes, which give an attacker administrator privileges,

   possible.
