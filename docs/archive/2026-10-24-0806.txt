   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [supply chain, breach, credential
   theft]
   Latest developments: LiteLLM disclosed on Friday, October 23,
   2026 that a configuration file committed to the public

   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, exploit]

5. BROTLI DECODER FLAW REACHES BUN, PYTHON, AND ENVOY
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [vulnerability, patch, supply
   chain]
   Latest developments: Researchers established that
   CVE-2026-111497, the memory-corruption bug behind this week's
   Node.js remote code execution, sits in Google's Brotli
   decoder itself and therefore in Bun, the Python brotli and
   brotlicffi packages, and Envoy proxies configured to
   decompress Brotli.
   Crafted Brotli-compressed data overwrites memory past the end
   of a buffer, and any service that unpacks attacker-supplied
   Brotli streams inherits the flaw. Inventory every runtime,
   package, and proxy that decodes Brotli rather than treating
   the Node.js 22.x, 24.x, and 26.x updates as the end of the
   work.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/brotli-decoder-flaw-behind-nodejs-rce.html

   Policy and Regulation · [policy, critical infrastructure]
   Latest developments: The Department for Digital, Culture,
   Media and Sport published a White Paper this week proposing

   fines for ship owners whose vessels cause the damage.

   reporting and protection duties to accompany the sentencing
   changes.
