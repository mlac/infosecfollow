   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, linux]
   Latest developments: The threat intelligence firm Previdian

   mitigation steps the same day for multiple unpatched
   zero-days in its DGS-3130 series managed stackable switches;

   or a distribution package built from it immediately, apply
   D-Link's mitigations for DGS-3130 switches, and update Paint
   from the Store.
