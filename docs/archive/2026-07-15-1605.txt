   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [malware, cryptocurrency]
   Latest developments: Kaspersky's GReAT team detailed OkoBot,
   a malware framework running on Windows since April 2025 that
   injects seed-phrase phishing pages into the genuine Ledger
   and Trezor desktop apps to steal recovery phrases, pairing
