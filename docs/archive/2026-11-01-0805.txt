1. FBI PINS THE $6.43 BILLION BINANCE HEIST ON TRADERTRAITOR
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [apt, cryptocurrency, breach]
   Latest developments: The FBI, the United States Secret
   Service, and Internal Revenue Service Criminal Investigation
   formally attributed the theft of more than $6.43 billion in
   virtual assets to TraderTraitor, the North Korea-aligned
   group also tracked as Jade Sleet, Pressure Chollima,
   PUKCHONG, Slow Pisces, and UNC4899, replacing the private
   tracing firms' inference with the government's own finding.
   A compromised DevOps engineer's laptop let the attacker reach

   withdrawals, with no stolen wallet private key involved.
   Binance has frozen nearly $3 billion on-chain, Tether froze
   1.567 billion USDT, and Circle froze 800 million USDC;
   exchanges and custodians should audit the paths between
   developer endpoints and withdrawal services.

2. LXD AND MICROCLOUD FLAW RATED 10.0
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, cloud]
   Latest developments: The Hacker News reported on November 1,
   2026 that a critical flaw in Canonical's LXD and MicroCloud
   lets an attacker who holds nothing more than a trusted
   client's public certificate log in as that client over the
   HTTPS API, no private key required.
   LXD runs Linux containers and virtual machines, and
   MicroCloud clusters them; an attacker who reaches the API
   across the network acts with the impersonated client's rights
   over the cluster and the virtual machines on it.
   Administrators should patch, pull the API off untrusted
   networks, and review trusted client certificates.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/critical-lxd-and-microcloud-flaw-cvss.html

3. HIJACKED DEBUGBEAR SCRIPT DRAINED POLYMARKET TRADERS
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [supply chain, cryptocurrency]
   Latest developments: The Hacker News named the hijacked
   component on November 1, 2026 as the page-speed script
   Polymarket loaded from the outside firm DebugBear, said the
   malicious replacement sold signed-in traders' positions and
   forwarded the money on October 29, 2026, and reported that
   Polymarket is refunding about $2.7 million to 726 wallets
   while blockchain records show roughly $2.64 million reaching
   the attacker.

   attacker the browser session of every logged-in trader who
   loaded the site. Sites should pin, subresource-integrity
   check, or self-host any script that runs beside authenticated
   user funds.

4. NETAMLOADER HITS GOVERNMENT WINDOWS BUILDS IN ASIA
   Nation-State Activity · [apt, malware]
   Latest developments: Kaspersky detailed NetamLoader this
   week, a previously undocumented .NET assembly-loading
   framework at the end of an infection chain aimed at a custom
   Windows 10 build that government entities in Asia use
   exclusively, The Hacker News reported on November 1, 2026.
   The operators tailored the chain to a government-only
   operating system image and load their payloads as .NET
   assemblies in memory, which leaves little on disk for
   file-based detection. Defenders running bespoke Windows
   images should hunt for unexpected .NET assembly loading and
   injection in their own fleets.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/netamloader-malware-targets-government.html
