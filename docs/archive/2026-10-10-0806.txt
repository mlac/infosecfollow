   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [clickfix, malvertising,
   infostealer]
   Latest developments: BleepingComputer reported on October 9,
   2026 that attackers now buy Google search ads whose click
   URLs pass through legitimate Bing search-result redirects, a
   laundering step that carries victims to counterfeit Claude
