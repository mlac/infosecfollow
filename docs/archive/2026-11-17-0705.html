Latest developments: Kaspersky published both halves of its analysis of Operation ShadowCloud, a campaign it first identified in early 2026 against government networks in Asia and Africa, naming two previously undocumented backdoors—GitDoor, which takes orders through GitHub, and GraphDoor, which runs its command and control through Microsoft SharePoint—plus the shellcode families and plug-ins they load; Unit 42 disclosed a separate Southeast Asian espionage campaign built on the PerfectPioneer remote access trojan and the modular KUKURAT, which hide their traffic in HTTP/3 over QUIC.
