5. CHAOS GANG'S MSARAT ROUTES C2 THROUGH BROWSERS
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [ransomware, malware, c2]
   Latest developments: Cisco Talos exposed msaRAT, a new
   backdoor the Chaos ransomware group deploys that hides
   command-and-control by routing it through the victim's Chrome
   or Edge browser, using WebRTC over TURN to mask the
   attacker's IP while enabling arbitrary command execution.
   By living off the browser rather than beaconing directly,
   msaRAT blends its traffic into ordinary web activity and
   evades network detection. Defenders should watch for
   anomalous WebRTC and TURN connections originating from
   browser processes.
   - Cisco Talos: https://blog.talosintelligence.com/chaos-msarat-living-off-the-browser-to-build-covert-c2-channel/
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-msarat-malware-uses-chrome-edge-browsers-to-route-c2-traffic/
