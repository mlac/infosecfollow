3. MALICIOUS SIM CARDS HIJACK PHONES AND IOT MODEMS
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [mobile, iot, vulnerability]
   Latest developments: Tomasz Piotr Lisowski and Marius Muench
   of the University of Birmingham, working with Kristian Covic
   of Fuzzware, showed that a compromised SIM card can issue
   commands built into the cellular spec to 26 tested phones and
   modules, stealing files, downgrading connections to 2G, and
   running code inside the modems of electric-vehicle chargers,
   industrial routers, and car telematics units.
   The attack abuses a legitimate function of the cellular
   standard, so a malicious or hijacked SIM can take over the
   device holding it; cellular IoT gear faces the most exposure.
