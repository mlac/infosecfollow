   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [vulnerability, cpu, research]
   Latest developments: Two independent teams disclosed
   techniques that defeat current Spectre v2 defenses:
   researchers unveiled TONTOU, which leaks Linux password
   hashes, and MIT CSAIL's Daniel Trujillo and Mengjia Yan
   detailed INTERRUPT INJECTION, which times a hardware
   interrupt to re-poison a processor's branch predictor after

   Linux systems carry the most exposure and should track
   incoming microcode and kernel updates.
