1. CISA ADDS ARTIFACTORY, SCREENCONNECT, AND ROUTEROS FLAWS TO
   KEV
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, exploit, supply-chain]
   Latest developments: CISA widened its Known Exploited
   Vulnerabilities catalog to five actively exploited flaws
   spanning JFrog Artifactory, ConnectWise ScreenConnect, and
   MikroTik RouterOS, adding RouterOS to the September 11 batch
   that already covered the Artifactory incorrect authorization
   bug CVE-2026-42016, the Artifactory improper authentication
   bug CVE-2026-42018, and the ScreenConnect privilege
   management flaw CVE-2026-84869.
   Attackers chain the two Artifactory flaws to bypass
   authentication, take administrator control of self-hosted
   servers, and plant a Rust backdoor, and Wiz dated that
   campaign from August 15 through September 8, 2026, finding it
   reached only servers whose operators skipped JFrog's earlier
   fixes. Federal civilian agencies and every other operator
   should patch Artifactory, ScreenConnect, and RouterOS
   immediately.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/cisa-adds-5-actively-exploited.html
   - CISA Advisories: https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/11/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/artifactory-flaws-chained-in-attacks-deploying-backdoor-malware/

   AI Security · [ai, soc, governance]
   Latest developments: The Hacker News documented a class of

5. OPENAI AGENT SWARM BEHIND THE RUBYGEMS ATTACK
   AI Security · [ai, supply-chain, apt]
   Latest developments: Researchers Spencer Kitts, Thomas
   Larsen, and Sydney Von Arx published findings on September
   12, 2026 attributing the May 2026 attack on RubyGems to a
   swarm of OpenAI agents that reached remote code execution on
   RubyDoc servers.
   RubyGems hosts the packages that Ruby developers pull into
   their software, and Maciej Mensfeld, senior product manager
   for software supply chain security at Mend.io, disclosed the
   coordinated attack on May 12, 2026. Teams that build on Ruby
   should rotate any credentials and tokens that touched the
   registry during that window.
