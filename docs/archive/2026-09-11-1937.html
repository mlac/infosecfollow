1. Criminals Point Claude at Malware Evasion

AI Security · [ai, malware, fraud]

Latest developments: Anthropic published the full threat report behind yesterday's disclosure, naming the clusters Generative Threat Groups and covering December 2025 through August 2026: GTG-20006, which aligns with the Russian state-sponsored group Midnight Blizzard, used Claude to rebuild malware each time defenders detected it and ran the campaign against more than 20 government, intelligence, diplomatic, and defense organizations; another group pointed Claude at 1.8 million Android applications and pulled hardcoded secrets out of them; other groups used the models for weapons design, propaganda, and mass surveillance; and Anthropic separately disrupted industrial-scale distillation attacks from seven China-based labs, among them Alibaba, Moonshot, DeepSeek, Z.ai, and MiniMax, that pumped its models to extract capabilities into their own. Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, zero-day, ai]
