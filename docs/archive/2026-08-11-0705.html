Critical Infrastructure Security · [ics, critical-infrastructure, breach]

Latest developments: CERT Polska disclosed that a December 29, 2025 attack on a Polish combined heat and power plant marked the first observed case of intruders reaching an operational technology network through a private APN, the dedicated cellular link the local grid operator uses to reach remote equipment.
