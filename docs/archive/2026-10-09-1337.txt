   Identity and Trust Infrastructure · [pki, patch,
   post-quantum]
   Latest developments: Let's Encrypt said on October 8, 2026
   that it will cut the lifetime of its free TLS certificates to
   64 days beginning in February 2027, and Microsoft told
   customers the same week that devices running unsupported
