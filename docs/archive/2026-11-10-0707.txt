1. BARRACUDA GATEWAYS AND N-ABLE FALL TO PUBLIC EXPLOITS
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, exploit]
   Latest developments: Barracuda Networks warned on November
   10, 2026 that attackers now exploit the two Email Security
   Gateway flaws it patched November 2, 2026, among them
   CVE-2026-116606, an authorization bypass, to run commands as
   root, and N-able confirmed the N-central disclosure covers 33
   zero-days with working proof-of-concept exploits, a set the
   researcher calls Able2Shell.
   The Barracuda chain hands an attacker with no login full
   control of the appliance that filters a company's email;
   watchTowr published the working steps on November 5, 2026.
   N-able keeps its hosted N-central environments offline and
   tells on-premises customers to shut their own servers down,
   because the Able2Shell bugs allow remote code execution,
   credential theft, and the creation of administrator accounts.

3. TURLA'S CEREAL TOOLSET SIPHONS GOVERNMENT EMAIL
   Nation-State Activity · [apt, espionage, malware]
   Latest developments: ESET disclosed on November 10, 2026 that
   Turla, the Russia-aligned group also tracked as Snake,
   planted a previously undocumented modular .NET toolset
   researchers named Cereal on the Microsoft Exchange servers of
   two government organizations, one in Kazakhstan and one in
   Romania, and copied selected messages straight out of the
   mail flow.
   Cereal needs no attacker-owned command server: operators send
   instructions to the victim's own internet-facing Exchange
   server disguised as ordinary Outlook Web Access,
   Autodiscover, and ActiveSync requests, which leaves little
   outbound traffic to spot. Exchange administrators at
   government bodies should audit server-side modules and
   inspect those three request paths for anomalies.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/russian-turla-hackers-use-new-cereal.html
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/turla-hackers-use-new-ceral-malware-to-spy-on-govt-exchange-servers/
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/10/eset-turla-exchange-malware/
