1. PHP2Shell Exploitation Began Before Disclosure

Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, exploit]

Latest developments: Amazon's threat intelligence team, whose chief information security officer CJ Moses described the finding, said its MadPot honeypot service logged an exploit attempt from a commercial anonymization network on October 17, 2026, four days before the PHP Foundation disclosed CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, and broad scanning followed within hours of the patches; Palo Alto Networks confirmed that every version of PAN-OS and Panorama carries the flaws and has shipped no fixed release, Fortinet is checking 12 products and Cisco 31 products and cloud services, and OpenAI proposed a five-stage disclosure process for critical open-source bugs after Calif found one of the two flaws with the GPT-5.6 Sol model under OpenAI's Patch the Planet initiative and Wiz reported both independently.

read more

PHP2Shell is a pair of critical flaws in the PHP runtime that run an unauthenticated attacker's code on any server answering web requests, regardless of the application on top. CISA tells organizations to move to PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, or 8.2.35 immediately; customers of appliances that embed PHP, among them PAN-OS and Panorama, must wait on vendor builds and restrict management interfaces meanwhile.

4. APT41 Bootkit and Sandworm Wiper

Nation-State Activity · [apt, malware, wiper]

Latest developments: Kaspersky's Global Research and Analysis Team disclosed BlackX on October 22, 2026, a UEFI bootkit built along the lines of BlackLotus that the Chinese group APT41 revived against diplomatic organizations in Central Asia, exploiting the Baton Drop flaw CVE-2022-21894 in the Windows boot manager to run before the operating system loads; separately, researchers said on October 21, 2026 that the Russian state-linked group Sandworm worked with a second threat actor last year to destroy data at a Ukrainian grain logistics company.

read more

A UEFI bootkit loads ahead of Windows and survives a reinstall, which puts boot-chain integrity and firmware monitoring at the center of any response. Diplomatic missions in Central Asia and Ukrainian agricultural logistics firms face the two campaigns; both call for verified offline backups and checks that Secure Boot revocations for CVE-2022-21894 sit in place.
