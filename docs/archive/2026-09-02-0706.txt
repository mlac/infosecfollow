1. SONICWALL SMA 1000 ZERO-DAYS CHAINED FOR RCE
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, vpn]
   Latest developments: SonicWall confirmed attackers are
   chaining two previously undisclosed SMA 1000 flaws in the
   wild—CVE-2026-83548, a pre-authentication server-side request
   forgery rated a perfect CVSS 10.0, and CVE-2026-83549—to
   reach unauthenticated remote code execution, and shipped
   emergency updates.
   The SMA 1000 series is a line of SSL VPN remote-access
   appliances that medium and large enterprises, government
   agencies, and managed security service providers run.
   SonicWall's William Perry and Adam Babis found the bugs
   internally; administrators should patch immediately.

3. SALITY P2P BOTNET DISMANTLED AFTER 23 YEARS
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [botnet, takedown, malware]
   Latest developments: On August 31, 2026, authorities from the
   United States, Bulgaria, Hungary, and Romania, working with
   CrowdStrike and the Shadowserver Foundation, seized Sality's
   infrastructure by manipulating its peer lists and taking down
   payload URLs, cutting the operator off from more than 15,000
   still-infected machines.
   Sality first appeared in 2003 as a file-infecting virus and
   grew into a peer-to-peer botnet that spread additional
   malware for 23 years. The sinkhole operation ends the
   network's ability to push new payloads to victims worldwide.
