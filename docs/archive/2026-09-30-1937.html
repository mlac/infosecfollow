1. NetScaler Exploitation Yields WHIPSHOT and SLAPSHOT

Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, exploit]

Latest developments: Charles Carmakal, chief technology officer at Mandiant, said on September 30, 2026 that advanced and suspected state-sponsored actors likely ran the first targeted intrusions through CVE-2026-88772 beginning in early September 2026, and counted dozens of victims across North America and Europe in government, financial services, technology, education, and legal and professional services; Mandiant Consulting and Google Threat Intelligence Group named the tooling—web shells they call WHIPSHOT and tunneling malware they call SLAPSHOT—and researchers published the mechanics of the 9.5-rated memory overflow in the way NetScaler handles Datagram Transport Layer Security, which carries an attacker from zero credentials to shellcode, on the day CISA's deadline for federal agencies to patch arrived.

read more

Citrix fixed CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 in NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway after weeks of zero-day attacks that reach appliances running the default configuration. Attackers take root, harvest credentials, and pivot into internal networks, so patch the appliances, rotate secrets, and hunt for web shells and new accounts.

2. Cisco SD-WAN Manager Zero-Day Draws Attacks

Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, exploit, patch]

read more
