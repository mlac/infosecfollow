1. Binance Loses $6.3 Billion to a Laptop Compromise

Ransomware and Cybercrime · [cryptocurrency, breach, apt]

Latest developments: Binance confirmed the loss at about $6.3 billion on Thursday, October 29, 2026, raising the figure from the $5.8 billion it was probing a day earlier, and said in a security update early on October 30, 2026 that a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop let the attacker reach a service container and submit unauthorized withdrawals, with no evidence of a stolen wallet private key; Wired reported that the attacker moved the funds in roughly 20 minutes and that tracing firms expect billions to stay gone; the exchange has frozen nearly $3 billion on-chain, Tether froze 1.567 billion USDT and Circle 800 million USDC, and blockchain investigators tie the theft to TraderTraitor, the North Korean crew behind earlier exchange heists.
