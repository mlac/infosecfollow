1. PASSKEY LURES AND CEO IMPERSONATION HIT MICROSOFT 365
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [phishing, identity, fraud]
   Latest developments: Microsoft put numbers on the fraud wave
   on September 13, 2026, counting more than 1 million scam
   emails sent between August 3 and August 5, 2026 by operators
   who posed as chief executives and pushed the messages through
   third-party email delivery services, a campaign running
   alongside the passkey and single sign-on social engineering
   that ShinyHunters, Helix, and other extortion crews aim at
   corporate Microsoft accounts.
   The passkey lures talk employees into enrolling an
   authentication method the attacker controls, which yields
   durable access to Microsoft 365, SharePoint, OneDrive, and
   mail; the invoice and payment scams stack legitimacy tricks
   with AI help. Administrators should review recent
   authentication method enrollments and restrict which outside
   senders may deliver mail under the company's domain.

5. SOGOU INPUT METHOD FLAW DELIVERS GRAYRABBIT
   Nation-State Activity · [apt, backdoor, exploit]
   Latest developments: BleepingComputer identified the bug
   behind the China-aligned espionage campaign on September 13,
   2026 as CVE-2026-51990, a critical flaw in Tencent's Sogou
   Input Method for Windows that operators exploit to plant the
   GrayRabbit backdoor.
   Sogou Input Method ranks among the most widely used ways to
