2. FORTIOS, F5, JUNIPER, AND OFFICE DRAW FIXES
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, malware, zero-day]
   Latest developments: A suspected advanced actor exploited
   CVE-2025-61624, a path traversal flaw in the command line
   interpreter of FortiOS, FortiPAM, FortiProxy, and
   FortiSwitchManager, to deliver PEERSTITCH, a memory-resident
   ARM64 malware that bypasses checks on tagged VPN sessions; F5
   and Juniper Networks patched critical flaws that reach remote
   code execution and full device takeover; Microsoft closed two
   dozen remote code execution bugs in Office plus several
   information disclosure flaws and a security bypass; and
   Microsoft said its KB5127648 Windows 11 security update may
   stop users from turning Smart App Control on.
   PEERSTITCH lives only in memory, so a reboot erases it and a
   forensic image of disk shows nothing, and the Fortinet bug it
   rides carries a modest 5.4 severity score. Administrators
   should patch FortiOS, F5, Juniper, and Office now, and
   Windows 11 users who cannot enable Smart App Control after
   October's update should treat the control as off.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-exploit-fortios-flaw-to.html
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/f5-juniper-networks-patch-critical-vulnerabilities/
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/microsoft-patches-two-dozen-rce-flaws-in-office/
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/kb5127648-windows-security-update-may-break-smart-app-control/

3. BEYONDTRUST ZERO-DAY OPENS REMOTE SUPPORT SERVERS
   Nation-State Activity · [zero-day, apt, patch]
   Latest developments: BeyondTrust told customers on Thursday,
   October 15, 2026 to patch CVE-2026-102764, a 9.3-rated
   improper origin validation flaw in an internal messaging
   component of its Remote Support product, and researchers tied
   the zero-day exploitation to Armory Castle, the China-nexus
   espionage group they previously tracked as UNC5221 and Warp
   Panda, which used the bug for initial access in late August
   2026, installed custom tools, and then deleted logs and
   artifacts to cover its path.
   BeyondTrust Remote Support, the product organizations once
   bought as Bomgar, gives help desks privileged access to
   employee machines, so a takeover of the server hands an
   attacker that same reach. Administrators running Remote
   Support should apply the fix and hunt for deleted logs and
   unexplained sessions dating to August 2026.

4. BAE SYSTEMS ATTACK HALTS FEDERAL BANKRUPTCY NOTICES
   Critical Infrastructure Security · [breach, supply-chain]
   Latest developments: The Administrative Office of the United
   States Courts said a cyberattack on the contractor BAE
   Systems knocked out the notice system that federal bankruptcy
   courts use, halting all production of bankruptcy notices.
   Bankruptcy notices tell creditors, debtors, and trustees when
   filings, hearings, and deadlines land, and the courts
   outsource their production to BAE Systems. Parties in
   bankruptcy cases should expect no mailed or electronic
   notices until the Administrative Office restores the system,
   and should track dockets directly.

5. BOTNETS DRIVE 9.1 MILLION DDOS ATTACKS
   Critical Infrastructure Security · [ddos, botnet]
   Latest developments: Netscout counted more than 9.1 million
   distributed denial-of-service attacks worldwide between
   January and June 2026, up nearly a quarter year over year,
   with attacks above 1 terabit per second or 1 billion packets
   per second climbing 1,286%, growth the firm traces to the
   botnets Aisuru, Kimwolf, and Jackskid and their successors,
   which knock services down before traffic fills the target's
   links.
   The same consumer hardware feeds a parallel business:
   BleepingComputer documented a proxyjacking campaign that
   hijacks D-Link and Ubiquiti devices in 34 countries and
   resells the victims' bandwidth through a commercial
   residential proxy network. Owners of edge routers and access
   points should update firmware and close remote
   administration, and service operators should size defenses
   for packet rates rather than bandwidth alone.
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/16/netscout-ddos-attack-growth-report/
