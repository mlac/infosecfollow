3. HUMAN DISRUPTS BADBOX 3.0 AD FRAUD
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [botnet, fraud]
   HUMAN's Satori Threat Intelligence and Research Team
   disrupted BADBOX 3.0, the backdoored low-cost TV box
   operation that peaked near 40 billion ad bid requests a day.
   Why it matters: The same backdoors rent out owners' home
   internet connections as residential proxies, handing
   criminals clean IP addresses for attacks on other targets.
   - By the numbers: The fraud topped 4 percent of all connected
     TV traffic HUMAN observed and more than tripled VASTFLUX,
     the previous record holder.
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/23/badbox-3-0-ad-fraud-operation/

5. ATTACKERS EXPLOIT ASP.NET ANTI-CSRF LIBRARY FLAW
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch]
   Researchers told developers to abandon the legacy
   Idunno.AntiCsrf library after attackers exploited
   CVE-2026-131914, rated 9.3, for unauthenticated remote code
   execution.
   Why it matters: Any ASP.NET application still loading the
   library hands attackers code execution with no credentials,
   and the component sits deep inside older web stacks.
   - What to do: Find and remove Idunno.AntiCsrf.dll versions
     0.9.1.0 and 0.9.1 Beta. Researchers recommend discontinuing
     the library rather than waiting for a fix.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-critical-aspnet-anti.html
