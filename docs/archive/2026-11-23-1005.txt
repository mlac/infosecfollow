1. ATTACKERS RUN ROOT COMMANDS ON CISCO EXPRESSWAY
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, zero-day]
   Russian security company F6 reported that attackers exploited
   CVE-2026-76466 in Cisco Expressway gateways to breach
   organizations and reach their internal networks.
   Why it matters: Both Expressway and SAP NetWeaver sit at the
   enterprise network edge, so root-level command execution
   gives intruders a staging point for internal movement.
   - What to do: Install Cisco's Expressway and SAP's NetWeaver
     updates now. CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities listing
     sets a remediation deadline for federal civilian agencies.
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/sap-netweaver-cisco-expressway-flaws-exploited-in-attacks/
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-flags-critical-cisco-expressway-rce-flaw-as-actively-exploited/
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/23/cisco-expressway-vulnerability-exploited-cve-2026-76466/

2. GL.INET PATCHES CLOUD FLAW AFTER BACKDOORS
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [malware, patch]
   GL.iNet fixed the GoodCloud remote management flaw on Friday
   and blocked the attacker's access, and says cleaning infected
   routers and KVM devices requires new firmware.
   Why it matters: Affected owners reported reverse shells and
   backdoors on their hardware, so a reboot or factory reset
   leaves the implant running.
   - What to do: Reflash affected GL.iNet routers and KVM
     devices with the new firmware. Closing the GoodCloud path
     stops fresh infections only.
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/gl-inet-routers-kvms-compromised-via-remote-management-service-flaw/
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploited-glinet-cloud-flaw-to.html
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-glinet-cloud-flaw-to-infect-routers-kvm-devices/

3. CERT-IN WARNS OF AI-ASSISTED BANK FRAUD
   AI Security · [apt, breach]
   The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team said AI-assisted

4. ATTACKERS HIJACK MSP TECHNICIAN HOSTS
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, breach]
   Threat actors exploited ConnectWise ScreenConnect flaw
   CVE-2026-84869, rated 9.9, against a managed service provider
   server on November 12, 2026, running payloads on a connecting
   technician's machine.
   Why it matters: A hijacked technician host hands intruders
   remote access to every customer that provider manages,
   turning one compromise into many.
   - What to do: Patch ScreenConnect clients against
     CVE-2026-84869. N-able fixed CVE-2026-134624, a server-side
     request forgery bug in Mail Assure that high-privilege API
     users could aim at internal systems.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-critical-screenconnect.html
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/n-able-discloses-critical-mail-assure.html

6. HUMAN DISRUPTS BADBOX 3.0 AD FRAUD
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [botnet, fraud]
   HUMAN's Satori Threat Intelligence and Research Team
   disrupted BADBOX 3.0, the backdoored low-cost TV box
   operation that peaked near 40 billion ad bid requests a day.
   Why it matters: The same backdoors rent out owners' home
   internet connections as residential proxies, handing
   criminals clean IP addresses for attacks on other targets.
   - By the numbers: The fraud topped 4 percent of all connected
     TV traffic HUMAN observed and more than tripled VASTFLUX,
     the previous record holder.
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/23/badbox-3-0-ad-fraud-operation/
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/badbox-3-0-ad-fraud-operation-generating-40-billion-daily-bid-requests-disrupted/

7. DISTRIBUTORS KEEP ARTEX ONLINE AFTER TAKEDOWN
   AI Security · [ai, apt]
   404 Media found multiple people redistributing the ARTEX AI
   hacking framework for anyone to download after its original
   author apparently pulled the project offline.
   Why it matters: Removing the upstream repository no longer
   limits access, so defenders should expect continued
   ARTEX-driven intrusions from criminal crews and state groups.
   - Flashback: CERT-EU identified ARTEX as the tool behind the
     European Commission intrusion disclosed last week.
   - 404 Media: https://www.404media.co/the-code-is-already-copied-everywhere-the-ai-hacking-tool-causing-havoc-around-the-world-artex-github/

8. DRAGOS FINDS PROMPT THEFT AT INFERENCE PROVIDERS
   AI Security · [ai, breach]
   Dragos uncovered undetected intrusions at three AI inference
   providers after examining nearly two million prompts users
   sent to AI services.
   Why it matters: Prompts carry source code, credentials, and
   customer records, so a silent breach at an inference host
   exposes everything its customers submit.
   - Between the lines: The providers never spotted the
     intrusions themselves, leaving their customers unaware that
     prompts were walking out the door.
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/ai-inference-providers-failed-to-detect-hacking-and-prompt-theft-says-dragos/

9. ATTACKERS EXPLOIT ASP.NET ANTI-CSRF LIBRARY FLAW
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch]
   Researchers told developers to abandon the legacy
   Idunno.AntiCsrf library after attackers exploited
   CVE-2026-131914, rated 9.3, for unauthenticated remote code
   execution.
   Why it matters: Any ASP.NET application still loading the
   library hands attackers code execution with no credentials,
   and the component sits deep inside older web stacks.
   - What to do: Find and remove Idunno.AntiCsrf.dll versions
     0.9.1.0 and 0.9.1 Beta. Researchers recommend discontinuing
     the library rather than waiting for a fix.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-critical-aspnet-anti.html
