1. ITALY AND SWITZERLAND FLAG AUTONOMOUS AI ATTACKS
   AI Security · [ai, critical-infrastructure]
   The Swiss and Italian national cyber agencies warned that
   attacks driven by autonomous AI agents are hitting critical
   infrastructure organizations in both countries.
   Why it matters: Agent-driven attacks compress reconnaissance
   and exploitation into minutes, outrunning patch cycles and
   human-speed triage at utilities, transport, and manufacturing
   operators.
   - What to do: Unit 42 tells teams to baseline normal behavior
     and push detection to the edge.
   - What's next: Google will require Workspace AI agents to use
     Model Context Protocol or command-line access, with agent
     identities and human approval, starting February 2027.
   - What to watch: Cyber insurers now split clients into
     AI-defense haves and have-nots.
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/italy-and-switzerland-warn-of-autonomous-ai-driven-cyberattacks/
   - Unit 42 (Palo Alto): https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/ai-threat-horizon/

2. DRUPAL PATCH GAP LEAVES SITES EXPLOITABLE
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, exploit, web]
   Drupal disclosed CVE-2026-136716, a highly critical flaw
   already exploited in the wild, because its earlier
   Fail2Install fix left some sites in vulnerable installation
   states.
   Why it matters: Site owners who applied the first patch and
   declared victory still face unauthenticated takeover across
   government, university, and media deployments.
   - What to do: Update Drupal core a second time. Hunt for
     unexpected site reinstalls, new administrator accounts, and
     changes to settings files.
   - Flashback: Attackers have exploited the original bug,
     CVE-2026-132165, since at least November 19, and CISA set a
     November 27 deadline for federal agencies.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/drupal-warns-of-new-highly-critical.html
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/drupal-installer-patch-left-a-gap-attackers-are-already-exploiting/
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/25/drupal-installer-rce-cve-2026-132165-exploited/

3. ATTACKERS EXPLOIT MITEL MICOLLAB ZERO-DAYS
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, exploit]
   Accenture said hackers exploited Mitel MiCollab flaws before
   Mitel published its advisory, likely with AI assistance, in a
   suspected extortion campaign.
   Why it matters: MiCollab servers carry voice, chat, and
   directory data for entire organizations, and the attacks ran
   while no patch existed.
   - By the numbers: CISA added 10 flaws to its Known Exploited
     Vulnerabilities catalog on Wednesday, among them five
     MiCollab bugs plus Juniper, Vates, and Danfoss products.
   - What to do: Patch MiCollab and audit the Audio, Web and
     Video Conferencing component for uploaded files under
     CVE-2026-133275. Pull management interfaces off the
     internet.
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/mitel-micollab-zero-days-exploited-in-suspected-ai-assisted-attacks/
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-10-vulnerabilities.html
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-critical-unpatched-flaw-in-danfoss-refrigeration-controllers/

5. GOODGUYS PHISHING STEALS GOOGLE AND OKTA LOGINS
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [phishing, identity]
   A phishing campaign dubbed GoodGuys impersonates Google,
   Nvidia, Accenture, and SpaceX with fake paid expert interview
   invitations to steal corporate Google and Okta credentials.
   Why it matters: Stolen single sign-on credentials hand
   attackers an employee's mail, files, and every connected

     invitations as phishing. Enforce phishing-resistant
     authentication on Google and Okta accounts.
   - By the numbers: FortiGuard Labs counted more than 24,000
     newly registered shopping-themed domains over 90 days,
     4,400 of them malicious.

6. SYS010 MALWARE HIJACKS BROWSER ENTERPRISE MANAGEMENT
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [malware, infostealer]
   A new version of the SYS01 infostealer, SYS010, spreads
   through Meta ads and enrolls victim browsers into
   enterprise-style management to force-install a data-stealing
   extension.
   Why it matters: The forced extension reads Gmail and Outlook
   mail, turning an ad click into persistent mailbox
   surveillance with no user consent prompt.
   - The big picture: A second stealer, Remus, pulls Gmail
     messages through a fake Google Sheets Chrome extension its
     operators marketed as a Gmail zero-day.
   - What to do: Alert on new browser cloud management policies
     and enrollment profiles appearing on endpoints, and block
     extension installs outside an approved list.
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sys010-malware-abuses-browser-management-to-spy-on-gmail-outlook/
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/remus-malware-steals-gmail-messages-via-fake-google-sheets-extension/

8. ATTACKERS EXPLOIT JUNIPER SESSION SMART CONDUCTOR
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [exploit, patch, network]
   Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-103406 in Juniper Session
   Smart Conductor to run arbitrary commands as root, and CISA
   added the flaw to its exploited catalog Wednesday.
   Why it matters: Conductor sits at the center of Session Smart
   router deployments, so root command execution there exposes
   the network paths it manages.
   - What to do: Patch Session Smart Conductor now and keep its
     management interface off the public internet. Review
     Conductor hosts for unexpected root processes and new
     accounts.
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/critical-juniper-session-smart-conductor-flaw-exploited-in-attacks/

9. NORTH KOREA'S POLINRIDER POISONS GITHUB REPOSITORIES
   Software Supply Chain Security · [supply-chain, apt, malware]
   SafeDep reported that the North Korea-linked PolinRider
   campaign planted a Python loader in more than 7,600 GitHub
   repositories, with evidence the activity started by February
   2025.
   Why it matters: Developers who clone and run a poisoned
   project hand attackers code execution on machines holding
   source code, cloud keys, and signing credentials.
   - Zoom in: The loader hides inside a small utils package or
     in conftest.py, so it fires the moment Python imports the
     file.
   - What to do: Scan repositories for unexpected conftest.py
     files and utils packages, and run untrusted projects only
     in disposable virtual machines.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/north-korea-linked-polinrider-poisons.html

10. KEENADU MALWARE SHIPS INSIDE ANDROID FIRMWARE
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [malware, mobile]
   Kaspersky found the Keenadu malware family preinstalled in
   the firmware of more than 220,000 Android devices during the
   first ten months of 2026.
   Why it matters: Firmware malware survives factory resets and
   runs before first boot, so a new personal phone can reach
   corporate mail already compromised.
   - By the numbers: Keenadu and the older Triada family
     accounted for 88% of infected devices, and monthly
     detections grew more than 2.5 times over the past year.
   - What to do: Buy handsets from known vendors through
     authorized channels, and keep unmanaged budget Android
     devices off single sign-on and corporate mail.
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/26/keenadu-malware-android-devices/
