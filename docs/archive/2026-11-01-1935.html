1. FBI Pins the $6.43 Billion Binance Heist on TraderTraitor

Ransomware and Cybercrime · [apt, cryptocurrency, breach]

Latest developments: The FBI, the United States Secret Service, and Internal Revenue Service Criminal Investigation formally attributed the theft of more than $6.43 billion in virtual assets to TraderTraitor, the North Korea-aligned group also tracked as Jade Sleet, Pressure Chollima, PUKCHONG, Slow Pisces, and UNC4899, replacing the private tracing firms' inference with the government's own finding.

read more

A compromised DevOps engineer's laptop let the attacker reach a Binance service container and submit unauthorized withdrawals, with no stolen wallet private key involved. Binance has frozen nearly $3 billion on-chain, Tether froze 1.567 billion USDT, and Circle froze 800 million USDC; exchanges and custodians should audit the paths between developer endpoints and withdrawal services.

3. LXD and MicroCloud Flaw Rated 10.0

Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, cloud]

Latest developments: The Hacker News reported on November 1, 2026 that a critical flaw in Canonical's LXD and MicroCloud lets an attacker who holds nothing more than a trusted client's public certificate log in as that client over the HTTPS API, no private key required.

read more

LXD runs Linux containers and virtual machines, and MicroCloud clusters them; an attacker who reaches the API across the network acts with the impersonated client's rights over the cluster and the virtual machines on it. Administrators should patch, pull the API off untrusted networks, and review trusted client certificates.
