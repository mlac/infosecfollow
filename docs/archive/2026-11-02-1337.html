3. Google Dismantles the XCPE Android Botnet

Ransomware and Cybercrime · [botnet, android, takedown]

Latest developments: Google and industry partners seized the servers behind XCPE in late October 2026, breaking a botnet of more than 1.3 million enrolled Android devices, most of them television set-top boxes, and Google's Threat Intelligence Group attributes the operation with high confidence to Videostrong Technology, the manufacturer that built the backdoor into the firmware and sold criminals access to its customers' home networks for advertising fraud and traffic proxying; Google has banned the company.

read more

XCPE lived in the firmware of cheap Android TV boxes before a buyer ever opened the carton, so owners could neither see it nor remove it. Anyone running one of these boxes should unplug it from the network, and buyers of low-cost streaming hardware should stick to devices certified under Google's Play Protect program.
