1. NGINX, Plex, and FortiGate Under Fire

Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, zero-day, exploit]

Latest developments: F5 broke its schedule on October 19, 2026 and shipped out-of-band patches for several NGINX web server flaws, three of them critical bugs that run an attacker's code; attackers weaponized the Plex2Shell exploit chain against Plex media servers within hours of its disclosure, dropping cryptocurrency miners and proxy software; and researchers linked the already-exploited Fortinet flaw to a stealthy, persistent implant that one intruder planted on a FortiGate firewall at a telecommunications company.

All three products sit at the network edge, where NGINX fronts much of the web, Plex servers face the internet by design, and FortiGate firewalls terminate remote access.
