   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [exploit, patch, zero-day]
   Latest developments: SecurityWeek reported on October 1, 2026
   that attackers exploited the Zimbra flaw CVE-2026-73570
   before anyone disclosed it publicly, and that a crafted email
   triggers the bug under certain conditions with no user
   interaction; Kiteworks released updates the same day for 126
   vulnerabilities, among them a maximum-severity code injection
