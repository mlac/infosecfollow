1. CRYPTO WALLET ATTACKS
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [crypto, supply-chain,
   vulnerability]
   Latest developments: Coinspect disclosed Ill Bloom, a
   weak-randomness flaw in wallet recovery-phrase generation
   that attackers used to drain more than $5 million starting
   with a coordinated May 27 sweep, Ledger's Donjon team showed
   a precisely timed laser pulse resets the password on Tangem
   wallet cards that cannot be patched, and threat actors who
   compromised Injective Labs' GitHub pushed a malicious npm
   package, @injectivelabs/sdk-ts@1.20.21, that steals wallet
   keys and seed phrases.
   Cryptocurrency holders face converging attacks on the seed
   phrases and hardware cards that control their funds. Owners
   should verify wallet software versions, avoid the compromised
   Injective SDK build, and treat any recovery phrase generated
   by weak software as already burned.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/attackers-exploit-ill-bloom.html
