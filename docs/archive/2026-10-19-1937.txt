1. CONNECTWISE SUSPENDS CLOUD SERVICES
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [breach, credentials]
   Latest developments: Sophos detailed on October 19, 2026 how
   automated sign-ins used ConnectWise's Entra application
   identity to request Microsoft Graph tokens across every
   tenant that had consented to it, a cross-tenant token sweep;
   ConnectWise kept Cloud Backup, the service it formerly sold
   as SkyKick, along with SaaS Security and Cloud Management
   offline through remediation and security validation, and says
   customer impact remains unconfirmed.
   Attackers used a compromised credential belonging to the
   ConnectWise Cloud Backup application identity to sign in to
   customer Microsoft 365 tenants, 173 of them by researchers'
   count. Managed service providers and their customers should
   audit Microsoft 365 sign-in logs and application consent
   grants for ConnectWise identities and arrange interim backups
   while the services stay down.
   - Help Net Security: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/19/connectwise-cloud-backup-suspended-security-incident/
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/connectwise-suspends-cloud-services-in-response-to-cyberattack/
   - Sophos News: https://www.sophos.com/en-us/blog/connectwise-cloud-backup-application-abused-in-token-sweep
   - The Record: https://therecord.media/connectwise-suspends-cloud-backup-service-supply-chain-attack

2. NGINX, PLEX, AND FORTIGATE UNDER FIRE
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, zero-day, exploit]
   Latest developments: Unit 42 updated its threat brief on
   October 19, 2026 to say the ransomware operators it tracks as
   Spikey Scorpius, known publicly as Qilin, and Tarnished
   Scorpius, known as Inc, now exploit PAN-OS CVE-2026-0257; F5
   broke its schedule the same day and shipped out-of-band
   patches for several NGINX web server flaws, three of them
   critical bugs that run an attacker's code; a researcher using
   the alias boredhackerblog found attackers chaining four Plex
   Media Server flaws, the set called Plex2Shell, to drop
   cryptocurrency miners and reverse proxy software a day after
   the proof of concept went public; and researchers linked the
   already-exploited Fortinet flaw to a stealthy, persistent
   implant one intruder planted on a FortiGate firewall at a
   telecommunications company.
   All four products sit at the network edge, where NGINX fronts
   much of the web, Plex servers face the internet by design,
   and PAN-OS and FortiGate firewalls terminate remote access.
   Patch NGINX, Plex, and PAN-OS now, and hunt FortiGate devices
   for the implant and for deleted logs; a clean firmware
   version proves nothing by itself.
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/f5-releases-out-of-band-patches-for-critical-nginx-vulnerabilities/
   - SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/plex2shell-exploit-chain-weaponized-against-plex-servers-hours-after-disclosure/

3. POISONED PLUGINS AND PACKAGES SPREAD BACKDOORS
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [supply chain, backdoor,
   wordpress]
   Latest developments: Rank Math said on October 16, 2026 that
   copies of Rank Math PRO version 3.0.123 downloaded from
   rankmath.com or delivered as updates between 20:02 UTC on
   October 15 and 07:12 UTC on October 16 carried a
   self-restoring backdoor; OpenSourceMalware found the

   AI credits are bait.

4. AD BIDDING DELIVERS SPYWARE AS PEGASUS HITS SERBIA
   Nation-State Activity · [spyware, zero-click]
   Latest developments: Dark Reading named the technique
   AdFection on October 19, 2026: spyware vendors buy targeted
   impressions in the digital ad auction to fingerprint and
   track individuals and organizations of interest, then infect
   the selected handset the moment it renders the ad;
   researchers found Pegasus on the phone of the rector of the
   University of Belgrade, the latest in a run of such
   discoveries among Serbian opposition figures ahead of
   national elections; and a federal judge denied WhatsApp's bid
   to hold NSO Group in contempt of a 2025 injunction barring it
   from using the messaging app to spread spyware, saying
   WhatsApp hurt its own case by withholding information.
   Mercenary spyware makers now reach a target through the same
   real-time advertising auctions that serve ordinary apps and
   websites, which requires no click and leaves little trace.
   Journalists, politicians, and civil society staff should
   expect zero-click delivery through ads, and the organizations
   that employ them should block ads and trackers on managed
   phones and run periodic forensic checks.
   - Dark Reading: https://www.darkreading.com/data-privacy/adfection-online-ads-spyware-delivery
   - The Record: https://therecord.media/serbia-spyware-pegasus-elections
   - The Record: https://therecord.media/whatsapp-denied-appeal-alleged-nso-group-violation-injunction
