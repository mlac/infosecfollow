   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [android, malware, banking]
   Latest developments: BleepingComputer reported July 12, 2026
   that a new RedHook variant abuses Android Wireless Debugging,
   or Wireless ADB, to gain shell-level privileges on a phone
   without any computer connection.
