1. FalconFlank Zero-Day Hits CrowdStrike Falcon

Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, endpoint]

Latest developments: The researcher known as Chaotic Eclipse, also called Nightmare Eclipse, published a proof-of-concept for FalconFlank, a privilege-escalation zero-day that abuses the malicious-macro remediation routine in the CrowdStrike Falcon Sensor, the same researcher whose HardBreacher exploit forced a Kaspersky patch on August 31, 2026; Cisco patched CVE-2026-20212, a CVSS 9.8 bug in 10 Silicon One-based Nexus 9000 switches that gives an unauthenticated remote attacker root code execution, shipped an IOS XR hardening release bundling seven CVEs with no workaround for any version, and left publicly disclosed S/MIME flaws in Secure Email unpatched; Hewlett Packard Enterprise patched a critical remote-code-execution flaw in its ArubaOS-CX network operating system, and Plex told users to update desktop clients and media servers immediately.

read more

FalconFlank turns a defensive feature of the Falcon endpoint agent into a path from a local account to full control of the host. The unpatched Cisco Secure Email defects can expose the contents of encrypted mail; apply the Nexus, IOS XR, ArubaOS-CX, and Plex updates now and watch for a CrowdStrike sensor release.
