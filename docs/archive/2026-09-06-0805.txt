1. MIKROTIK ROUTERS FALL TO UNAUTHENTICATED SSH ATTACKS
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [exploit, network, zero-day]
   Latest developments: CERT Polska published an attack warning
   on September 5, 2026, reporting that attackers take full
   administrative control of MikroTik routers whose SSH service
   answers from the internet, with no authentication required
   and successful compromises dating to at least September 2,
   2026.
   MikroTik routers sit at the edge of small-business, hosting,
   and branch-office networks, so administrative control hands
   an attacker traffic interception and a pivot into everything
   behind the device. CERT Polska has released no victim count;
   pull SSH off the public internet, limit management access to
   known addresses, and audit routers for added accounts,
   scripts, and scheduled tasks.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/attackers-hijack-mikrotik-routers.html

2. REVSTEALER MODULES KILL DEFENDER AND MINE CRYPTO
   Ransomware and Cybercrime · [malware, cryptomining]
   Latest developments: Elastic Security Labs documented four
   previously unreported programs that ride along with
   REVSTEALER, an emerging Windows information stealer, and stay
   on the machine after the stealer deletes itself, among them
   ProManager, WinUpdate, and SoftManager; one of the four
   disables Windows Update and Microsoft Defender before
   launching a cryptocurrency miner.
   REVSTEALER harvests credentials and then erases its own
   binary, which has masked the persistent modules that remain
   behind on Windows hosts. Treat a REVSTEALER hit as an ongoing
   compromise, check whether Windows Update and Defender have
   been turned off, and hunt for the named components.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/four-revstealer-linked-modules-disable.html

3. CRITICAL VMWARE WORKSTATION AND FUSION OVERFLOW
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, virtualization]
   Latest developments: Broadcom assigned the critical desktop
   hypervisor bug CVE-2026-59346 at CVSS 9.3, an integer
   overflow that a local attacker holding elevated privileges
   inside a guest exploits to run arbitrary code, and shipped
   fixes for it alongside a second Workstation and Fusion flaw.
   VMware Workstation and Fusion run on developer, researcher,
   and analyst desktops, where malware analysts rely on the
   guest boundary to hold. Apply the Broadcom updates, and stop
   treating an off-the-shelf virtual machine as containment for
   hostile code.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/critical-vmware-workstation-and-fusion.html

4. RESEARCHERS CATALOG 39 PASSKEY ATTACKS
   Identity and Access Management · [authentication, research]
   Latest developments: The authentication vendor Token
   published 39 documented methods for compromising
   passkey-based logins, all of which abuse authentication
   prompts, synced credentials, enrollment, and account recovery
   while leaving FIDO2 cryptography intact.
   Passkeys close off password phishing and credential stuffing,
   and the surrounding workflows still decide who gets in.
   Organizations deploying passwordless authentication should
   harden enrollment and recovery paths, restrict credential
   syncing across devices, and treat prompt approval as an
   attackable step.
   - BleepingComputer: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/39-new-methods-that-compromise-passkey-authentication/

5. KREMLIN HARDENS DATA CENTERS AGAINST DRONES
   Nation-State Activity · [policy, critical infrastructure]
   Latest developments: The Kremlin has imposed new physical
   security requirements on Russian data center operators, whose
   facilities cluster in regions that Ukrainian drone strikes
   now reach.
   Russia concentrates its commercial and government computing
   in a handful of geographic areas, and drone range has turned
   that concentration into a resilience problem that firewalls
   do nothing about. The rules push operators toward physical
   hardening of buildings, power, and perimeter.
   - The Record: https://therecord.media/russia-data-centers-ukraine-drone-threats
