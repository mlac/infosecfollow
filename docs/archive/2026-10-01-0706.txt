1. CISCO CATALYST SD-WAN MANAGER ZERO-DAY
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [zero-day, patch, exploit]
   Latest developments: Cisco shipped fixed releases and
   indicators of compromise for CVE-2026-76504 on October 1,
   2026, and said its incident responders learned of live
   exploitation in September 2026 while working a Cisco
   Technical Assistance Center support case, the fifth time this
   year attackers have used an SD-WAN zero-day against Cisco

   Binding Operational Directive 26-04; patch the Manager and
   hunt against Cisco's published indicators.

5. NETSCALER PAYLOAD MINTS A SUPERUSER
   Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [exploit, zero-day, web-shell]
   Latest developments: The Threat Hunt Operations and Research
   team at LevelBlue reported on October 1, 2026 that it traced
   NetScaler exploitation across multiple customer environments
   to a post-exploitation payload that creates a superuser
   account and maps a web shell to URLs resembling stylesheet
   requests, while attackers also tried to steal appliance
   configuration data.
   The pre-authentication command injection flaws CVE-2026-88771
   and CVE-2026-88772 in Citrix NetScaler ADC and NetScaler
   Gateway have carried attackers from zero credentials to root
   since early September 2026, and Palo Alto Networks Unit 42
   updated its threat brief on September 30, 2026. Patch, then
   hunt for unexpected superuser accounts and web shell paths
   that masquerade as CSS.
   - The Hacker News: https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/citrix-netscaler-post-exploitation.html
   - Unit 42 (Palo Alto): https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/netscaler-zero-days-exploited/
