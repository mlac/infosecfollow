Latest developments: Binance's first full incident report, detailed November 4, 2026, traces the $6.34 billion theft to a rigged coding assignment handed to engineers during fake recruitment interviews and counts three engineers' computers compromised, one more than the exchange described a day earlier, with the attackers also abusing compromised internal AI tooling before reaching the wallet infrastructure; North Korea fired missiles toward the sea the same day while protesting United States claims that it ran the heist.
