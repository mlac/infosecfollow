7. Flash Chip Attack Breaks TPM Lockout

Platform Security · [hardware, encryption]

Latest developments: Researchers at Technische Universität Berlin showed that anyone who opens the case of an AMD-based PC, and of some Intel PCs, can reset the TPM lockout that throttles PIN guessing, and in lab tests they recovered a four-digit Windows Hello PIN on an AMD laptop and a six-digit BitLocker PIN on an Intel laptop in days.

read more

Short Windows Hello and BitLocker PINs rely on the TPM refusing repeated guesses, and physical access to the flash chip removes that limit. Organizations issuing laptops should require long PINs or pre-boot passphrases and treat a lost machine as a machine an attacker can open.
