4. Sshole Exploit Attempts Climb

Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [exploit, patch]

Latest developments: The SANS Internet Storm Center reported on November 8, 2026 that its Cowrie honeypots log a rising count of attempts against CVE-2026-123158, the OpenSSH flaw researchers call sshole, and flagged its own blind spot: the sensors keep only the first 255 characters of an attempted SSH password, while the exploit needs one between 257 and 1,024 bytes.

read more

The flaw lets an attacker with network access, an account name, and an overlong password log into key-only or passwordless accounts on musl-based Linux systems such as Alpine. Administrators should move to OpenSSH 10.6p2 or their distribution's fixed package and treat long-password login attempts in their logs as exploitation.
