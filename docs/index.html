1. Armory Castle Owns the vCenter Zero-Day

Nation-State Activity · [zero-day, apt, patch]

Latest developments: Google Threat Intelligence Group attributed the zero-day exploitation of CVE-2026-123890 to Armory Castle, the suspected China-nexus espionage group formerly tracked as UNC5221, which has used the authentication bypass since at least early September 2026—more than two months before Broadcom released a fix—to take administrative sessions on vCenter Server and steal data.
