Social Engineering and Fraud · [phishing, social engineering, ai]

Latest developments: Microsoft said it will add AI-powered, context-aware phishing detection to Teams messages starting in early November 2026; Dark Reading reported on October 23, 2026 that attackers now skip malware and simply call corporate help desks to ask for access, with AI smoothing the performance; and the New York Knicks ran an internal phishing test that dangled a fake offer of championship rings in front of their own employees.

Help-desk social engineering walks around technical controls by getting a human to reset a password or an authentication factor for the caller. Any organization with an IT service desk should require out-of-band callback and ticket-based identity proof before any credential or multifactor reset.

2. Agents Watching Agents Draw Doubts

AI Security · [ai, insider threat]

Latest developments: Dark Reading examined Microsoft's Project Perception on October 23, 2026, which adds Guardian Agents that monitor and contain AI agents behaving anomalously, and reported the objection that policing unpredictable agents with other agents may compound the risk rather than contain it; SecurityWeek separately reported that banks have begun spotting rogue AI agents inside their own environments.

read more

Microsoft treats an AI agent as an insider and tightens its permissions the moment its activity strays from its assigned job. Organizations handing agents standing credentials should log every agent action to a system the agent cannot touch and cap each agent's permissions at the single task it performs.

Sources: Dark Reading · SecurityWeek · ↑ top

3. FCC Bars Devices Built on Huawei Chips

Policy and Regulation · [policy, supply chain, surveillance]

Latest developments: The Federal Communications Commission banned two devices, a wireless video transmitter and a dashboard camera, because both carry chips from HiSilicon, the Huawei subsidiary that markets its parts as designed for the surveillance market.

The commission controls which radio equipment companies may import and sell in the United States, and it now reaches past the brand on the box to the silicon inside. Buyers of cameras, dashcams, and video transmitters should check vendor bills of material for HiSilicon components before ordering.

4. Windows Server 2016 Loses Updates in January

Vulnerabilities and Exploits · [patch, end-of-life]

Latest developments: Microsoft confirmed that Windows Server 2016 stops receiving security updates when its support ends in January 2027.
